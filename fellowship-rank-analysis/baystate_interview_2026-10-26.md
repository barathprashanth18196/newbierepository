# UMass Chan–Baystate ID interview, 10/26/2026 (from Plaud, full transcript read)

Interviewers: Armando Paez (PD and chief), Durane Walker (APD), Delpilar-Morales (hospital epidemiologist, tropical medicine), Rahul (2nd-year fellow). Coordinator: Jonathan.

- **Size:** 1 fellow per year; 13–14 ID providers (3 APPs). Expansion to 2/year is stalled.
- **Schedule:** inpatient month alternates with elective/research month, both years (12 inpatient months over 2 years). Teaching service = fellow + attending + 1–2 residents + ID pharmacist; 2nd and 3rd services are attending/APP only.
- **Caps:** 4 new consults/day weekdays (shared with learners), none after 3 pm; 6 new on weekends. Fellows can pick the interesting consults before 10 am.
- **Call:** in each 4-week inpatient block, 2 weeks of daytime pager, 1 week of night pager (5 pm to 7–8 am, phone calls and drug approvals), weekend rounding with the attending. Pager covers Baystate Medical Center only; the attending covers the other hospitals. Attending always backs up.
- **Rotations:** transplant 1 month at UMass Worcester, paid (lodging too), second month possible if self-funded and in Massachusetts; peds ID 1 month (year 1); TB clinic (pulmonary-run); wound care; micro. Separate Ryan White HIV clinic (about twice the HIV numbers needed). Travel clinic closed since COVID.
- **Research:** clinical only, no bench. Scientific review committee with a data scientist, a Dept of Medicine research coordinator, IRB about 2 weeks for retrospective work. Fellows aren't sub-investigators on industry trials. No funded research fellowship. Possible mentor: an ID-trained, NIH-trained intensivist in PCCM. Research months: 3 clinics/week, the rest project time; at least 1 QI project required.
- **CCM:** PD said there is no combined ID+CCM (2+1) track; fellows have gone on to CCM sequentially. Early ICU collaboration helps that application.
- **Boards:** 100% first-time pass for 20+ years; 2nd-years get an expense account for the 5-day board review course in Virginia (August).
- **J-1 waiver:** Massachusetts Conrad slots are few and competitive; maybe 1–2 Baystate hospitalist waiver jobs a year, depending on the year. PD has waiver contacts in Georgia.
- **Money and life:** fellow pays about $2,000 for a furnished 2-bed in East Springfield (about 3.5 miles away); his first year was a shared room for about $950. Meal card about $250–300 every 3 months, plus a 24-hour lounge with free coffee and snacks. Conferences paid (flight and hotel) if an abstract is accepted.
- **Parking:** designated resident/fellow parking (covered garage plus an open back lot), "not an issue." The fellow didn't drive until recently, and nobody said whether it's free.
- **Community:** big South Indian community and restaurants (Walker, Rahul); diverse faculty; faculty retreat at the PD's home. No combined GME social events that the fellow knew of.
- **Girlfriend:** PD and faculty said she could transfer into Baystate family medicine; Baystate needs family doctors after the Mercy acquisition.
