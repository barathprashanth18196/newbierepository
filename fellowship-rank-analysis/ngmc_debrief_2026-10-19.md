## Program facts (PD presentation)
**Sites and units**
- Gainesville (629 beds) is the primary site. Braselton (236 beds) is a smaller community hospital. Fellows don't rotate at Habersham, Barrow or Lumpkin.
- **Two closed medical ICUs:** MICU (16 beds) and pulmonary ICU (24 beds). Each has 1 attending, 3–4 residents and a pharmacist; the pulmonary ICU also has an APP. A fellow is added to each.
- **Nights:** one team covers both ICUs (1 attending, 2 residents), and a fellow is added from year 2.
- Shifts are 7 AM–7 PM.
- **The ICU team owns all intubations,** rapids and codes included, except in the OR and ED.
- **Elective ICUs:** neuro ICU, CVICU, CT ICU and SICU/trauma. Fellows don't staff them.

**Rotations by year**
- **Year 1:** MICU and pulmonary ICU only, plus consults and bronchoscopy.
- **Year 2:** adds night float, neuro ICU and cardiac ICU.
- **Year 3:** career customization, leadership and advanced electives. The program supports Emory for lung transplant.
- **Consult team:** 1 attending, 1 APP, 1–2 residents, plus a fellow. Census is ~15 (30s in flu season). It does all inpatient bronchoscopy and manages trach patients outside the ICU.
- **Outpatient bronchoscopy:** 7 AM–5 PM with a separate attending.

**Bronchoscopy volume** (FY26, through Aug 31; two years earlier in parentheses)
- About 500 BALs (300)
- More than 300 transbronchial biopsies (150)
- About 350 EBUS
- More than 300 Ion robotic bronchoscopies

There is no IP fellowship, so fellows will do robotic bronchoscopy after an initial observing period. There's a bronchoscopy simulator.

**ECMO**
- Through August this year: 4 VV, 10 VA, plus ECPR.
- CV surgery cannulates now. The plan is for the ICU to take over VV ECMO, including cannulation, in about 1–2 years.

**Education**
- **Critical care echo:** a 2-year curriculum built to support the **CCE board**. "We want our fellows to take the board."
- **Simulation:** a quarterly half day. The 18-month curriculum runs twice over the fellowship (vascular access, thoracic procedures, airway/vent emergencies, shock…).
- **Academic half day:** Thursdays 1–4 PM, protected from clinical duties. It replaces noon conference and covers case, radiology/path, M&M, journal club, echo conference and vent rounds.
- **Continuity clinic:** a half day at the NGPG pulmonary office near the main hospital, with your own patient panel. It's paused during ICU and night-float months.

**Research and mentoring**
- No bench research. Retrospective chart reviews and QI projects.
- A **dedicated GME research division** helps with ideas, IRB, data extraction and analysis, manuscripts and posters.
- **Protected research time:** 2 weeks in year 1, and 6 weeks in each of years 2 and 3 (split up).
- A mentor is assigned at the start of fellowship.

**Money and faculty**
- **Money:** $3,000/yr education stipend plus $1,800/yr CME.
- **Faculty:** PCCM, interventional pulmonology, nephrology CC, surgical CC, EM/anesthesia CC, CC echo/sim (Dr. Pratik Juneja), ECMO and research/medical education.
- **Trachs:** Dr. Deshmukh, Dr. Devalla and Dr. Jim Bailey do percutaneous trachs. Certification is possible, but the case count still needs to be worked out.

**Plans and logistics**
- **Subspecialty clinics** (ILD, advanced lung disease, PH): the goal is by the end of year 2, with guaranteed exposure in year 3.
- **Thank-you emails:** not required and won't affect rank.
