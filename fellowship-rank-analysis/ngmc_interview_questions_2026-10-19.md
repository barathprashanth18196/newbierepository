# NGMC PCCM interview: questions to ask (10/19/2026, 8:30 AM–1:30 PM ET)

Trimmed with Barath on 10/19.

**Already settled, so don't ask:**
- No 24-hour call
- No moonlighting
- Leave, and interview or conference days
- 4 fellows per year
- Accreditation status
- The rotation grid (on the program website)
- Research setup
- No ongoing trials
- What the faculty want to build

## Ask
1. **Away rotations:** Which institutions would host each one?
   - Burn ICU
   - Transplant ICU
   - ILD
   - Can I also do a rotation with the anesthesia team (airway/OR intubations)?
2. **POCUS:** What does the curriculum look like? Is there image review or archiving, a path to credentialing, and support for the NBE critical care echo (CCE) exam?
3. **Board review:** Does the program pay for a board review course for pulmonary and critical care (e.g., the CHEST or SCCM review course) or for question banks? Is there time off to attend?

## Optional (Barath hasn't decided)
- Which ICUs fellows cover, at which campus, and whether there's a census cap
- ECMO: will fellows be on the ECMO team?
- Procedures: do fellows get priority over residents and APPs?
- Can elective time be weighted toward critical care?
- Does NGHS hire graduates into J-1 waiver jobs?
- What changes going from resident to fellow in the same hospital?

## Watch-outs
- **"Why pulmonary?"** At MCW I said pulmonology was never part of the plan, and Dr. Gagan Kumar (an MCW alum) works here. Have a real pulmonary answer ready.
- **Letters of recommendation:** have a one-liner on the oncology letters.
- **The oncology pivot:** keep it short and forward-looking.
- **Close strong:** say what I'd add as a first-class fellow.
