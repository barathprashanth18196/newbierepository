Screening notes:
- Affiliation matching used the affiliation text only. No author is surnamed "Tufts". Collective (group) authors were skipped so that the real last named author was used.
- 22 of the 35 kept papers are TUSM-affiliated but not from Tufts MC Boston: Maine Medical Center/MaineHealth (9, all listing TUSM), Lahey (2, as requested), plus outside sites (Mount Auburn, Italy, Hartford, and others) where one Tufts-affiliated author is first or last.
- Cardiogenic Shock Working Group (CSWG) analyses were excluded as registry studies under Barath's rule: about 20 papers, almost all with Kapur (Tufts cardiology) as senior author. REVEAL, COMPERA, ELSO, UNOS, Komodo claims, NIS, BRFSS and TriNetX papers were excluded for the same reason.
- CRIC-cohort papers (Tufts nephrology, 2) were kept as cohort studies but flagged. They are investigator analyses of a multicenter NIH cohort, which is a borderline case for "large secondary database."
- Three kept papers have no abstract (40183328, 39213019, 42642829), so their design was inferred from the title and author list. 42642829 is a 2-author item and may be a research letter.

Exclusions by reason: reviews/SR/MA 46; registry/database/claims 36; pediatric/neonatal 32; lab/animal/veterinary/simulation 29; case reports/series 20; editorial/commentary/letter without data 17; surgery/trauma/ortho/ENT/urology/OB-gyn 15; preprints 9; disparities 8; COVID 4; nutrition 2; policy/education 2.

## (c) Who leads the kept work

- **Tufts MC PCCM (Boston), 7 kept + 3 trials.** Nearly all of it is the **pulmonary hypertension group**: Nicholas Hill (3 kept + 2 trials), Harrison Farber (2 kept, both with Raj Parikh at Hartford as first author), Ioana Preston (SOTERIA trial; senior author on the Strick Group 3 PH paper, where she is now listed at Lahey), and Daniel Strick. Only two kept papers are ICU-focused. One is Hassan Chweich's Impella 5.5 stroke study, which comes out of the cardiac/shock ICU with the CardioVascular Center. The other is J. Zeitler's delirium/blue-light study, which has no abstract. The only ICU trial is the JAMA spontaneous-breathing-trial RCT, with Hill as senior author. No kept paper on sepsis/ARDS, ILD (apart from the PH-ILD work), sleep, or COPD is led from Tufts MC PCCM.
- 6 PCCM faculty appear as first or last author on kept papers or trials: Hill, Farber, Preston, Chweich, Strick and Zeitler. Strick and Zeitler may be trainees. Hill is listed as Chief Emeritus.
- **Much of PCCM's PubMed output was excluded:** reviews (Preston, Hill, McKenna/Hon, Pradhan), editorials (Ruopp/Farber, Hon/Farber), case reports/series (McKenna/Farber sotatercept, Strick/Hill, Chweich IABP), claims studies (Farber/Komodo, Medicaid), REVEAL (Ruopp), COVID HFNO (Tariq/Hill), and a language-disparities spirometry paper (Ghamloush, with McBrine among the coauthors).
- **Other Tufts MC departments account for most of the critical-care-adjacent clinical research:** the CardioVascular Center (Kapur, Hungerford, Gulati, Aslam; shock/MCS/PH hemodynamics, 6 kept + 2 trials, plus about 20 CSWG registry papers excluded); nephrology (McCallum, Sarnak, Tuttle; 4); anesthesiology (2); neurology/neurocritical care (Nelson; 2); hematology-oncology (2).
- **Maine Medical Center (TUSM)** has an active critical care research group: Seder, Gagnon, May, Wirth (PROTECT trial program, cardiac arrest, SAH, pharmacology). It is a separate institution in Portland, ME, not where a Tufts ID fellow would do the critical care year.

## (e) Bottom line vs Tufts ID

1. Tufts ID: 21 kept, 12 division-led, 7 of 38 faculty publishing. Tufts MC PCCM: 7 division-led kept papers (+3 trials) from 6 people. The 35 "kept" is a Tufts-wide count from a broader topic search, and the clinical research in it mostly belongs to cardiology, nephrology and MaineHealth.
2. PCCM's research engine is the pulmonary hypertension program (Hill, Farber, Preston: trials plus hemodynamic cohorts). ICU/critical care research led from the Boston division is thin: 2 kept papers and 1 RCT in two years, with no sepsis/ARDS papers and no T32 or NIH grant found.
3. Treat the critical care year as clinical training rather than a research platform. Shock/MCS research runs through Kapur's CardioVascular Center, and the PH leadership is in transition: Hill is Chief Emeritus and Preston has likely moved to Lahey. Ask the division about protected research time, the current chief, and ICU research mentors.
