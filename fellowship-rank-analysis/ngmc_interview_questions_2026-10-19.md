# NGMC PCCM interview: questions to ask (10/19/2026, 8:30 AM–1:30 PM ET)

Built from the ETSU (9/18) and MCW (9/16) Plaud transcripts, the Notion tracker and the rank-list open items. I couldn't read the program's curriculum and FAQ pages because this session's network blocks ngmcgme.org, so skip any question those pages already answer.

## 1. Must ask: these change the score
1. **Call:** How is overnight call set up? How many 24-hour shifts per month or block, in-house or home call, and is the next day off? *(Open item: call frequency. It's scored "unfavorable" until answered.)*
2. **Moonlighting:** Will fellows be allowed to moonlight, internally or externally, and from which year? How does that work for J-1 fellows? *(The tracker says No. Confirm it.)*
3. **Leave:** Is it 15 vacation days? Are conference and interview days separate?

## 2. A brand-new program
4. Where is accreditation now, and when is the first ACGME site visit? How many fellows per year?
5. Can I see the full three-year rotation grid? How many months are ICU vs. pulmonary vs. research vs. elective?
6. How many core pulmonary/CCM faculty are there, and is there an in-house attending at night? With no senior fellows yet, who backs up first-years overnight?
7. How will feedback from the first class shape the curriculum? Is there a fellow seat on the program evaluation committee?
8. What will change for me going from resident to fellow in the same hospital, with the same nurses and attendings?

## 3. ICU training (my Critical Care focus)
9. Which ICUs do fellows cover (MICU, pulmonary ICU, neuro ICU, CVICU), at which campus (Gainesville vs. Braselton), and is there a census cap?
10. **ECMO:** The program is just starting (3–4 patients at a time). Will fellows be on the ECMO team? Is there a plan to grow it, or an away rotation (ETSU sends fellows to Greenville, SC)?
11. **Transplant and oncology ICU:** Are away electives possible (e.g., Emory) for transplant or BMT/oncology critical care?
12. **Procedures:** Do fellows get priority over residents and APPs? What's the volume of bronchoscopy/EBUS, tracheostomy and chest tubes? How involved is the interventional pulmonology faculty?
13. **POCUS/echo:** Is there a structured curriculum and support for the NBE critical care echo (CCE) exam?
14. **Didactics:** What's the weekly schedule, board review, simulation lab, and protected conference time?
15. Can elective time be weighted toward critical care (e.g., CVICU, neuro ICU, ID, palliative)?

## 4. Research *(scored Research 3.33)*
16. How many months of protected research time, and in which year?
17. What support is there: biostatistician, research coordinator, IRB turnaround, data access (Epic/Cosmos, national datasets), and funding for CHEST/ATS/SCCM?
18. Are there active ICU or pulmonary clinical trials fellows can join? *(Goal: learn to run trials.)*

## 5. Career and visa
19. Does NGHS hire its own graduates into J-1 waiver jobs? Is there a retention plan?
20. Are graduates expected to practice both pulmonary and critical care, or is an ICU-only career supported?

## 6. Culture
21. What kind of fellow will thrive here?
22. What is the faculty most excited to build in the next three years?

## Watch-outs from ETSU and MCW
- **"Why pulmonary?"** At MCW I said pulmonology was "never part of the equation," and I told MCW I wasn't keen on NGMC's PCCM. NGMC faculty know MCW alumni (Dr. Gagan Kumar works in the NGMC ICU). Have a real pulmonary answer ready: pulmonary complications in immunocompromised, oncology and transplant patients; bronchoscopy/BAL for opportunistic pneumonia; ILD and pulmonary hypertension in the ICU.
- **Letters of recommendation:** Both programs flagged letters written for oncology. Have a one-line answer and name the critical care letter writer.
- **The oncology pivot:** Keep it to 2–3 sentences and forward-looking: pharmacotherapy and complex physiology led me to the ICU.
- **Close strong:** It's my home program, so say what I'd add as a first-class fellow (QI, the sepsis education project, research).
